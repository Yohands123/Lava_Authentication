# DESIGN.md: LavaAuth

The lamp is the interface. Every security event is something the wax does. Do not edit this file without asking; propose changes instead.

## References (design/)

| Reference | Take | Leave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic orange lamp | Slow heat-driven rise and fall, necks before splitting, warm glow | Bottle silhouette, orange-only palette |
| Neon vertical blobs | Glossy specular highlights, tiny satellite droplets, faint vertical light streaks, magenta→blue by height | Uniform density everywhere |
| Gradient metaballs | Gradients inside each blob, long thin necks, deep navy over flat black | Stock-poster centred title |

Blob colour comes from temperature, never randomness: hot magenta→coral, cooling violet, coldest electric blue.

## Typography

- Display: Unbounded 300 / 600. One headline per screen. clamp(56px, 12.5vw, 200px), line-height 0.92, letter-spacing -0.035em.
- Body: Inter Tight 400 / 500, 16–19px, line-height 1.6.
- Technical: JetBrains Mono 400, 12–13px, letter-spacing 0.02em. Hashes, bit counts, health.
- Big type, lots of empty space. Headlines anchor bottom-left; the lamp owns the rest.

## Materials

- Blobs: metaballs in a WebGL 2 fragment shader; field-gradient normals; lit from within and from below; soft gloss top-left.
- Bloom: currently a halo around blobs; a real two-pass bloom (threshold ≈ 0.7) comes in Phase 8.
- Grain: 3–4% animated.
- Glass: blur 24px, saturate 140%, rgba(20,10,40,0.22) tint, 1px glass-edge border, inset top highlight.

## Motion

- Easing cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). UI transitions 600–1200ms. Nothing under 300ms.
- Undisturbed, a blob crosses the screen in 20–40 seconds.
- Enter button breathes on a 4-second cycle (glow, not scale).
- Reduced motion: lamp at 15% speed, fades only, press-and-hold replaces stirring.

## Banned

Cards or solid panels · component libraries · gradient pill buttons · emoji · icon sets · spinners · dashboard grids · elevation shadows · pure white text · bouncy easing.
