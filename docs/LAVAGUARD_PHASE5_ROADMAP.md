## Priorities and acceptance criteria

1. **Approve a prospective study design.** Before recruiting anyone, define use case, target false-flag rate, independent-person sample-size/power plan, device/browser coverage, accessibility conditions, retention, withdrawal, and dataset access. Owner approval must explicitly cover recruitment/collection. No claim may substitute eight pipeline-minimum codes for statistical adequacy.
2. **Collect and audit independent observations.** Preserve one code across a person's devices and retries; document supervised/self-reported label confidence. Include incomplete/accessible/abandoned attempts and ordinary-browser versus automation-takeover conditions. Acceptance: consent-before-capture verified, all sources reviewed, known duplicates/run/replay lineages resolved, no invalid rows or cross-cohort identity links.
3. **Freeze the study before looking at final outcomes.** Predeclare participants, generator families, nuisance controls, split seed, metrics, candidate parameters, calibration procedure and one final-test decision. Acceptance: both labels in each cohort without reseeding, repeatable hashes, independent review of group graph, final lock retained, thresholds selected without final labels/features.
4. **Compare models and failure modes.** Run fixed baselines, ablations and unseen-family controls on development only. Acceptance: report all folds/errors, raw denominators, per-person and device outcomes, uncertainty, calibration, OOD and matched-acquisition effects. Only a prospectively powered, untouched final evaluation can support a deployment discussion. Correct all scientific claims if performance is weak; do not relabel or hide errors.
5. **Complete independent security/accessibility review.** Include forged aggregates/metadata, replay, storage bounds, withdrawal recovery, unknown devices, actual touch/pen/assistive hardware, reduced motion, slow hardware and passkey-only route. Acceptance: no weaker authentication and no behavioral barrier that blocks accessible sign-in.
6. **Measure realistic inference costs.** Current measurements are local Node/Python. Acceptance: approved controlled staging measures Worker CPU, tail latency, cold start, bundle/memory and browser frame time, with explicit rollback, no automatic promotion and unchanged mandatory passkeys.
7. **Request a concrete staging decision.** Prepare a reviewed disabled artifact, model/data cards, protocol replay checks, consent/retention controls, monitoring limits and rollback. New approval is required for a remote PR/push, staging deployment or telemetry. Production remains a later separate decision. Behavioral scores must not become cryptographic humanity claims or permanent bans.

Advanced sequence models, adversarial intelligence and online adaptation are research proposals, not shipped production capabilities. Revisit them only after the simpler independent baselines expose a measured need. No automatic retraining, online learning, third-party fingerprinting or remote data collection has been added.
