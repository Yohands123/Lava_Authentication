# Participant and coordinator templates — not an active study

These drafts need owner and applicable ethics/privacy review. They are not evidence of invitations, recruitment, consent or participation.

## Plain-language consent draft

We are studying an experimental lava-circle interaction. Taking part is optional. You can use your passkey without joining the study and can stop at any time. If you opt in before an attempt, this page can retain up to 1,024 normalized pointer positions/times and bubble observations, declared device/browser/control labels, random study codes and task outcome. Movement patterns and study codes can still be identifying. Browser automation hints are uncertain and do not identify who operates the browser.

There is no automatic upload. You can review data before deciding whether to download it for the approved coordinator. The proposed retention is 30 days in controlled local private storage. We do not include your account, passkey, camera or cookies in the study export. Local withdrawal clears page memory. Ask the coordinator to remove supported downloaded/derived copies using your study code; external copies or backups outside their control cannot be guaranteed erased. Models/results derived from withdrawn data must be invalidated and reviewed again. There is no validated human-detection accuracy or promised security benefit.

Do you understand the proposed recording and wish to opt in for this attempt? Declining leaves authentication available. Do not start capture before explicit agreement.

## Coordinator checklist

- Obtain actual owner/applicable ethics approval; freeze protocol/source/cohorts and data-access/retention responsibility before enrollment.
- Explain voluntary participation, identifying-data risk, export review, alternatives, stop/withdrawal and unknown accuracy. Use a random study code, never a real name/email in exports.
- Record actual supervision and consent chronology. Confirm device and shared-device lineage; separate pilot from prospective cohorts. Never label a script as human or infer labels from passkeys, CLEAR or WebDriver.
- Match bot/human app/cadence/task conditions, supervise the prescheduled attempts and retain abandonment/missing-outcome accounting. Physical touch/pen/assistive and human-takeover sessions require available consenting operators.
- Keep exports and supervision local/private, validate provenance, track known copies and schedule owner-managed retention review. Stop on transmission, auth, privacy or accessibility problems.
- Review withdrawal dry run, apply the participant's request, inspect partial-cleanup status and invalidate dependent results. Preserve final-use history.

## Suggested invitation draft

We are considering a voluntary study of an experimental lava-circle sign-in interaction. Participation does not improve or restrict your access and does not replace a passkey. The approved consent materials will explain what local interaction data may be retained, how to review it, and how to stop or withdraw. Please do not send personal information or data until an approved coordinator provides the final protocol.

## Debrief/withdrawal draft

This study cannot determine that someone is human or an AI agent. Any displayed result is an experimental policy outcome. You may stop now, keep your export private, or request removal of known controlled study copies using your random code. The coordinator will report completed versus partial cleanup and any known external-copy limitations. No passkey or existing authenticated session is revoked because you decline research.
