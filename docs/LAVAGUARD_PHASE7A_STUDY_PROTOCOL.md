# Phase 7A prospective study protocol

**Draft; not approved, recruiting or running.** No genuine participant has been enrolled. The versioned contract is [study-protocol.json](../ml/study-protocol.json); its owner, applicable ethics review and application-source hash must be approved/frozen before enrollment. Approval for coding or GitHub publication is not study approval. Institutional review, if applicable, is a prerequisite.

The primary unit is an independently supervised, consenting adult participant. The primary endpoint is any behavioral false flag within the first three prescheduled attempts under the frozen reference policy. Attempts do not become independent people. A missing or abandoned outcome remains unknown unless an observed flag already establishes the endpoint. Report the denominator, unknowns and missing-outcome range. Accessible alternatives and authentication outcomes are separate endpoints; a passkey or CLEAR never establishes humanity.

Use a separate pilot for mechanics, followed by prospectively untouched evaluation cohorts. A participant cannot cross pilot/prospective arms, even with a different device or session. Keep the same random study code across a person's devices, assign separate session codes, and declare shared physical-device lineage. Codes are pseudonyms, not verified unique identities or guaranteed anonymity. Eight codes could exercise plumbing; they cannot establish a safe low false-positive rate.

Draft minimums are 50 independent training codes, 300 calibration codes and 300 final codes, plus at least three held-out bot families. These are proposed engineering/precision gates, **not an approved recruitment target or sufficient proof of representativeness**. Under independent Bernoulli assumptions, zero errors in 300 independent units gives a one-sided 95% upper bound about 0.994%; eight gives about 31.2%. Correlated participants, devices, selection and missingness can invalidate that interpretation. The exact calculation is tested in [phase7a.py](../ml/phase7a.py). Review design effect, expected event rate, precision and recruitment feasibility before changing the preregistration, never after inspecting final scores.

Capture people and declared scripts using the same frozen app source, original circle, 32-ms research cadence, 1,024-sample cap and advisory model setting. Touch, pen, assistive input, ordinary virtual browsers, remote-desktop use and human takeover of automation browsers are planned consenting study arms. Their physical tests are **NOT TESTED**, not simulated volunteers. Do not infer any of those environments from headless hints. No sensitive demographic or disability details are collected by this implementation; a justified extension would need separate review/consent.

Supervision records must bind every attempt to current consent, pre-sample chronology, participant/session, arm, declared device lineage, app source and cohort. Bot records additionally bind generator family/seed/run/path lineage and script/source/model lineage. Unknown labels stay unknown; incomplete attempts are retained for outcome accounting. Exact trace/vector duplicates, linked devices, sources or generators across frozen cohorts stop evaluation.

The primary policy is regularized logistic regression with fit-only preprocessing and calibration-only threshold selection. Random Forest and HistGradientBoosting are fixed development comparisons. The empirical calibration target of 1% is per attempt, not a promise about the participant endpoint or population safety. Freeze the reviewed candidate hash and numerical threshold before the exclusive final lock is reserved. No reranking, threshold tuning or model promotion follows final results.

Pause immediately on participant request, unexpected transmission, authentication regression, unexplained accessibility exclusion or unexplained false flags. Retention is proposed at 30 days with owner-managed review/deletion, not an active background deletion service. Follow [data governance](LAVAGUARD_PHASE7A_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md), [evaluation procedures](LAVAGUARD_PHASE7A_EVALUATION_PLAN.md) and [participant templates](LAVAGUARD_PHASE7A_PARTICIPANT_MATERIALS.md).
